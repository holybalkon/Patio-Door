Choosing a patio door

It depends on your opening and how you use the space. Sliding doors suit tight areas because the panels do not swing; French and hinged doors give a wide clear opening; bifold and stacking systems open up a whole wall; tilt-and-turn offers flexible ventilation. In Calgary's climate, prioritise energy-efficient glazing and good weather sealing regardless of style.

Ask for a patio-door-only written quote that lists the exact door model, frame material, glazing (for example double or triple pane and Low-E), hardware, installation, removal and disposal of the old door, and the full warranty terms with exclusions. Whole-project estimates that bundle windows and doors make it hard to compare patio-door value.

Third-party estimates vary by material and scope. Home Depot Calgary cites a Canadian average of about CAD 2,100 before tax for an installed two-panel sliding patio door. A Calgary installer lists installed 2026 ranges of roughly CAD 800 to 1,800 for standard sliding, CAD 1,500 to 2,800 mid-range, CAD 2,500 to 4,500+ for premium large formats, and CAD 3,500 to 8,000+ for multi-panel systems. These are not company quotes; request a written estimate.

Treat advertised warranty terms as company-stated until you see them in writing. Ask for the term length, whether it is transferable, what is excluded, whether labour is covered and for how long, and how glass seals and hardware are handled. A long headline number means little without the detailed conditions.
