Publisher & disclosure

Independent Expert is an independent comparison platform covering patio door replacement in Calgary, Alberta. We are not any of the companies we list, and our pages are not a ranking produced by a review platform such as Google.

Commercial disclosure

Our featured #1 pick is an editorial / featured placement. Where a listed company is also a client or has a commercial relationship with us, we disclose it here plainly. An editorial ranking that involves a client-controlled relationship must not be presented as an independently conducted study — and we do not present it that way.

What we did not do: we did not test doors in a laboratory, we did not make secret-shopper purchases, we did not measure installer response times, and we did not independently re-fetch live platform ratings beyond the dated snapshot described below.

Selection methodology

We started from a list of Calgary companies that supply and install patio doors, drawn from a third-party business directory and the companies' own websites. For the comparison on the home page we focused on ten companies with public rating data (Google ratings).

  1. Collect publicly available facts: product lines, stated warranties, addresses and hours from company sites.
  2. Record Google ratings from a single third-party directory so the figures are consistent.
  3. Give the featured pick a full editorial review, and give every other company a formula-based score calculated from the public data above (see “What the scores mean”).
  4. Tag every company claim we could not independently verify as company-stated.

What the scores mean

Two different numbers appear across this site, and we keep them separate on purpose.

Editorial score
Our opinion

A desk-research score out of 5. The featured pick has a full editorial review; the other companies have a formula-based score from public data. Not a Google rating and not proof of market leadership.

Google snapshot
Third party

Platform ratings with their own count and source. Snapshots change over time.

Formula-based score (all other companies)

For companies without a full review, the score out of 5 is calculated from public data only: 50% Google rating, 20% review volume (1,000+ reviews = 5, 400+ = 4, 200+ = 3, 75+ = 2, fewer = 1), 20% range of patio-door styles listed (4+ styles = 5, 3 = 4, 2 = 3, a patio category without named styles = 2, windows-focused = 1) and 10% warranty clarity (a clear term for doors = 5, a lifetime or exceptional claim with unclear scope = 3, a program mentioned without terms = 2, none stated = 1). The result is rounded to one decimal. These scores are not hands-on tests and do not cover pricing or service quality.

Sources & review policy

Our figures come from a small, named set of public sources, each with a date:

  • Google ratings and review counts — a dated snapshot that covers windows and doors broadly, not patio doors specifically.
  • Company websites — for product lines, stated warranties, addresses and hours.

Review policy

We reproduce customer reviews only with attribution, a star indicator and a link to the original, opening in a new tab with rel="nofollow noopener". We do not cherry-pick or edit sentiment. Pricing, warranty and feature claims are presented as company-stated, and we advise readers to get a written quote.

Corrections

Accuracy matters to us. If you represent a listed company — or you are a reader who spots an error — you can ask us to correct or update any detail on this site.

Send the page URL, the specific detail you believe is wrong, and a public source we can check to corrections@bestcalgarypatiodoors.online. We review correction requests and update dated snapshots as needed.

This is a research-stage comparison. Google ratings, warranties, addresses, hours and pricing may change over time.
